Confederation Equipment Borrowing Policy

Guidelines on custody, reservations, liability, and equipment care.

1. Eligibility & Accreditation

All recognized, accredited student organizations, academic councils, special-interest student clubs, and official university committees are eligible to borrow confederation-owned assets and stage equipment. Individual students may request items for academic presentations or recognized co-curricular activities provided they present a valid university ID and faculty endorsement.

2. Reservation Timeline & Gate Pass

Requests must be filed online through the Confed Borrowing Portal at least twenty-four (24) hours prior to the scheduled activity. For major events requiring canopy tents, sound systems, or large chair batches, early reservation of three (3) working days is strongly advised.

Upon approval by the Confederation Custodian, an official Equipment Gate Pass Slip will be generated. The borrower must present this printed or digital gate pass at the Student Activity Center Room 204 during pickup.

3. Inspection & Physical Release Protocol

During equipment release, both the Releasing Custodian and the Borrower must inspect all items (including cables, connectors, remote controls, and cases) for existing cosmetic or functional defects. Signing the release slip confirms that the equipment was received in good and operational condition.

4. Borrowing Duration & Overdue Penalties

  • Standard maximum borrowing duration is five (5) calendar days, unless an extension is formally granted in writing by the Confederation Executive Board.
  • Items returned beyond the expected return date without prior notice will incur a status of Overdue.
  • Failure to return assets on schedule will result in temporary suspension of borrowing privileges for the delinquent organization.

5. Liability for Damages or Loss

The borrower and the represented organization assume complete fiduciary and custodial responsibility. In the event of loss, theft, liquid damage, physical breakage, or burn-out due to negligent handling:

  • The organization must replace the asset with an exact model or equivalent unit approved by the Custodian within seven (7) working days.
  • Alternatively, the organization may cover the official repair cost certified by an authorized service center.

Need Special Equipment Allocation?

Visit the Confederation Main Office at Student Activity Center, Room 204 or contact confed.borrowing@university.edu.
